# Architecture decisions and trade-offs

**Goal:** Make technical decisions explicit, reversible where possible and measurable.

1. Frame the decision and its drivers: reliability, security, performance, cost, developer experience, constraints and expected scale.
2. Compare viable choices including the current system, with assumptions and factual evidence for each.
3. Evaluate failure modes, data migration, vendor dependencies, operating burden and expected maintenance costs.
4. Choose a proportionate solution and identify the conditions that would change the decision.
5. Write an ADR with context, options, decision, rationale, consequences, migration steps and review date.
6. Validate decisions through a prototype or measurement when feasible; do not overstate untested claims.

**Output:** Decision record, risk register, implementation checkpoints and rollback plan.
