# Landing page and conversion design

**When activated:** Apply this only if the actual task matches the description. First inspect available evidence and select relevant references; do not infer project details from the skill name.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, offer, acquisition intent and credible proof; establish the one primary action for each section.
2. Design the story from first impression through value, differentiators, proof, objection handling and clear next step.
3. Use an accessible visual hierarchy and responsive layouts; keep copy specific and verify every claim and testimonial.
4. Reduce form friction, maintain consent and privacy transparency, and avoid dark patterns or misleading urgency.
5. Measure usability, accessibility, performance and conversion with defined baselines instead of promising rankings or uplift.

## Deliverable
Page structure, copy hierarchy, component list and measurement plan.

## Quality gate
Report what was **observed**, what is **proposed**, which checks **passed/failed/were not run**, and any remaining risks. Use tools only when available and authorised. For destructive, financial, production or customer-data changes, require explicit approval. Never claim a test, screenshot, design file, deployment or tool action exists without evidence.
