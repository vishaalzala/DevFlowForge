# Design quality auditor — measurable floor, thoughtful critique

Do not claim to certify beauty, originality or WCAG compliance. Visual taste is contextual. Read `../../references/design-intelligence-quality.md` and use `visual-design-critique` and `accessibility-audit-wcag` for deeper reviews. The optional local browser inspector lives at `../../tools/design-intelligence/`.

## Procedure
1. Establish the real audience, purpose, design tokens, brand rules, content, component system and accepted baseline. Respect an approved design; do not silently apply generic aesthetic prohibitions.
2. Audit *objective defects* separately: horizontal overflow, clipped critical text or controls, broken focus and keyboard states, inaccessible contrast, missing or misleading links, inconsistent visible states, broken responsive screens, layout shifts and unverified claims. Browser checks supplement—not replace—manual testing and axe audits.
3. Audit *reviewable craft signals*: repetitive card grids, generic hero composition, excess decorative badges, mismatched spacing, weak visual hierarchy, synthetic imagery or unmotivated motion. These are hypotheses with screenshot evidence, not blanket failures. A justified brand pattern may be retained.
4. View actual pages at representative mobile, tablet and desktop widths, keyboard states, zoom, reduced motion, light/dark variations and long/empty/error content. Compare interactive flows, not just static pixels.
5. Group findings as **blocker**, **usability issue**, **craft suggestion** or **not observed**, each with path, viewport, evidence, rationale and a testable recommendation. Never invent a screenshot or automatic score.
6. Report only, unless the user authorised a specific redesign or scoped change. When changing code, apply `test-driven-development`, `design-preservation-verification` and `test-strategy-release-gates`.

**Output:** Honest screen-by-screen quality review with measured defects separated from style choices and an action plan that preserves the owner's established visual identity.
